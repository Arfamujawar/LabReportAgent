Your first report, step by step

Follow this workflow to see how Lab Report Agent supports you in preparing for a conversation with your doctor.

01 — Upload your report

Sign in, open the report upload page, and select your lab report in PDF, PNG, or JPG format.

02 — Review extracted values

Check each test name, result, unit, and reference range. Correct or verify information wherever the application allows it. Never assume an uncertain value is correct.

03 — Explore explanations

Read the educational explanations and compare your results with the reference ranges printed on your report. These explanations are not a diagnosis.

04 — Prepare questions for your doctor

Note the results or medical terms you want to discuss with a qualified healthcare professional.

05 — Save your visit-preparation summary

If needed, export the available summary and take it to your appointment. Your doctor remains the decision-maker for interpreting results and recommending care.

Privacy and security

Reports stay in this application's local database and upload folder, and only the account that uploaded a report can open it. In this build no report data is sent to an external AI service. Read the details .

Questions

Does it diagnose anything? No. It explains terms and compares values with the range on your report. It never diagnoses or suggests treatment.
Is a real AI model used? Not yet. Explanations come from built-in educational text and are labelled as demo mode.
Does it read scanned reports? Only if Tesseract OCR is installed on the machine running the app.
