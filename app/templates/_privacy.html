Privacy and security

  • Uploaded files and extracted values are stored on the machine running this app (SQLite database and an uploads folder).
  • No report data is sent to any external service in this build.
  • Passwords are hashed. Forms are CSRF-protected. Sessions expire after 2 hours.
  • Each report can only be opened by its owner. Uploaded files are stored unencrypted.
  • This is a prototype and makes no claim of HIPAA, GDPR, or other regulatory compliance.
